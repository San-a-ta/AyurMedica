# 🌿 AyurMedica — BAMS Student Digital Companion
### Standardized as per Ministry of AYUSH & NCISM (National Commission for Indian System of Medicine)

Welcome to **AyurMedica**, an all-in-one educational web platform crafted specifically for **BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery)** undergraduate scholars across all four Professional Academic Years.

---

## 🚀 How to Run the Platform

### Option 1: One-Click Local Server (Recommended)
Open your terminal in this directory and execute:
```powershell
python serve.py
```
This starts the local web server at `http://localhost:8000` and automatically opens the platform in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

### Option 2: Direct Browser Launch
Double-click `index.html` or drag and drop `index.html` directly into any web browser. No installation or build steps required!

## 🏛️ Platform Features & Architecture

### 1. 🎓 Academic Year Categorization
- **1st Professional Year (Foundation & Pre-Clinical)**:
  - *Kriya Sharira* (Physiology - Prakriti assessment, Dhatu Sara, Nadi, Hematology)
  - *Rachana Sharira* (Anatomy - 107 Marmas, Osteology, Dissection landmarks)
  - *Padartha Vijnanam evam Ayurveda Itihasa* (Fundamental principles & Pramanas)
  - *Samhita Adhyayana - 1* (Ashtanga Hridaya Sutrasthana & Charaka Purvardha ch 1-12)
  - *Samskritam* (Classical Medical Sanskrit grammar & shloka translation)
- **2nd Professional Year (Para-Clinical & Pharmacology)**:
  - *Dravyaguna Vijnana* (Materia Medica & Herbarium identification of 150+ medicinal plants)
  - *Rasa Shastra & Bhaishajya Kalpana* (Alchemy, Shodhana, Bhasma Pariksha, Kwatha/Vati/Taila)
  - *Roga Nidana & Vikriti Vijnana* (Diagnostics, Ashtavidha & Dashavidha Pariksha, Pathology)
  - *Samhita Adhyayana - 2* (Charaka Purvardha - Nidana, Vimana, Sharira, Indriyasthana)
- **3rd Professional Year (Clinical Specialties)**:
  - *Agada Tantra & Vyavahara Ayurveda* (Toxicology, Snakebite/Visha, Forensic Medicine, Injury certification)
  - *Swasthavritta & Yoga* (Preventive Health, Dinacharya, Ritucharya, Shatkarma, Dietetics)
  - *Prasuti Tantra & Stri Roga* (Obstetrics & Gynecology, Garbhini Paricharya, Yoni Vyapad)
  - *Kaumarbhritya / Bala Roga* (Pediatrics, Neonatal assessment, Swarnaprashana)
  - *Samhita Adhyayana - 3* (Charaka Samhita Chikitsasthana, Kalpa, Siddhisthana)
- **4th / Final Professional Year (Clinical Practice & Surgery)**:
  - *Kayachikitsa* (Internal Medicine - Jwara, Amavata, Prameha, Vatavyadhi, Shamana/Shodhana)
  - *Panchakarma* (Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, Raktamokshana clinical protocols)
  - *Shalya Tantra* (Surgery, 101 Yantras, 20 Shastras, Ksharasutra ligation in Bhagandara)
  - *Shalakya Tantra* (ENT, Ophthalmology, Akshi Tarpana, Anjana, Supra-clavicular disorders)
  - *Research Methodology & Medical Statistics* (AYUSH-GCP guidelines, Biostatistics)

### 2. 🧪 Practicals & Interactive Viva Lab
- **Step-by-Step Laboratory Procedures**: Apparatus, reagents, classical principles, and clinical precautions.
- **Interactive Checkbox Checklist**: Track laboratory steps in real time on mobile or tablet during practical sessions (saved automatically to your browser).
- **Interactive Viva Voce Cards**:
  - Contains high-frequency exam viva questions.
  - "Show Answer" toggle button for active self-testing.
  - "Toggle All Viva Answers" master switch for rapid group review.
  - *Examiner Scoring Tips* revealing what university professors look for.

### 3. 🎥 Live Practical Demonstrations & Clinical Theatre
- Interactive video simulation console with chapter markers and progress tracking.
- Timed clinical chapters (e.g. *Akantha Pana*, *Vamaka Yoga administration*, *Antiki Lakshana*).
- Clinical procedure checklists and clinical safety pearls from premier AYUSH institutions (NIA Jaipur, AIIA New Delhi, IMS BHU).

### 4. 📝 Previous Year Question (PYQ) Bank & Model Answers
- Filterable by:
  - **10 Marks (LEQ)**: Long Essay Questions with comprehensive structure: Definition, Classical Shloka, Classification, Pathogenesis (Samprapti), Clinical Features, Treatment Sutra, and Modern Correlates.
  - **5 Marks (SEQ)**: Crisp, high-yield answers with bullet points and comparison matrices.
  - **2 Marks (SAQ)**: High-yield definitions, numbers, and cardinal signs.
- One-click **"Copy Answer"** button for notes and assignments.
- Clean **Print-Ready Styles**: Click the print icon to generate clean PDF study sheets without web headers.

---

### 5. 📖 Easy Subject Summaries & Mindmaps
- Quick 5 to 8-minute pre-exam revision notes.
- Master comparison matrices (e.g., Tridosha properties, 6 Rasas & Mahabhutas, 5 Kashaya Kalpanas, 6 Seasons/Ritucharya, Panchakarma table).
